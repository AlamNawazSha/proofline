# Changes from the Round 1 plan

## Kept exactly
- The Evidence–Version Contract: no release without a completed review bound to the exact content revision and the approved source pack.
- Human-in-the-loop correction (accept / rewrite / contest) rather than silent auto-fixing.
- Approved sources first: upload alone never makes text authoritative.
- Honest evidence language: "no supporting evidence" is not "false".

## Delivered in Round 2 (beyond the original plan)
| Area | Round 1 plan | Round 2 build |
|---|---|---|
| Claim checks | qualitative "unsupported claims" | structured conditions — maximum value, "up to", audience, product scope, guarantee words |
| Required wording | not specified | trigger → required qualification rules |
| Rule quality | not specified | rule laboratory (sandbox) and rule-change impact preview |
| Privacy | general confidentiality | confidential-information preflight that blocks external AI |
| Suggestion safety | not specified | regression preview: numbers, removed qualifiers, remaining findings |
| Terminology | simple term list | variants, hyphen/space tolerance, section and channel scoping, expiry |
| Governance insight | planned as an idea | rule-clarity counts and resolution feedback from real findings |
| Bundles | not specified | campaign bundles that block unless every item is currently eligible |

## Brought forward from Round 3, with reason
Visual/accessibility checks and the Rule Clarity Report were originally Round 3. They were pulled forward because a governance product that cannot discuss assets or recurring rule confusion looks incomplete to a reviewer. The **depth** stays in Round 3: perceptual logo matching, sampled contrast, alt-quality guidance and trend history.

## Narrowed or deferred (stated honestly)
- No OCR; scans are rejected rather than half-read.
- No semantic logo understanding; an 8×8 average hash with distortion detection, always requiring human confirmation.
- No legal-compliance claim; rules are organisation-configured.
- No immutable/signed audit ledger; fingerprints identify content, they are not signatures.
- No multi-reviewer quorum, no automatic re-review queue, no live AI measurement.
- Supabase integration written but not executed against a live project.
